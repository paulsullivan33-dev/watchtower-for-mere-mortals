  $ WATCHTOWER_COOLDOWN_DELAY=banana ./watchtower --run-once
  level=fatal msg="Failed to load configuration" error="cooldown-delay: invalid duration \"banana\": invalid character in duration string: 'b' in \"banana\" at position 0"

  $ WATCHTOWER_POLL_INTERVAL=banana ./watchtower --run-once
  {"level":"fatal","error":"interval: parse int: strconv.Atoi: parsing \"banana\": invalid syntax","message":"Failed to apply environment configuration"}

So chapter 3's WATCHTOWER_POLL_INTERVAL (for --interval) and
recipe 2's WATCHTOWER_COOLDOWN_DELAY (for --cooldown-delay) are
the fork's real variable names, not guesses.
